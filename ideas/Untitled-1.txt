#23. SAFE FAILURE DESIGN

- A collector crashes
- A command is unavailable
- A platform is unsupported
- A tool times out
- A response action fails
- Verification fails

The system should:

1. Record the failure.
2. Explain the failure.
3. Continue unaffected monitoring where possible.
4. Never pretend the action succeeded.
5. Never automatically retry dangerous actions indefinitely.
